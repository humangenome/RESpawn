# RESpawn privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

RESpawn is a browser add-on for www.reddit.com. It has no servers of its own. It collects nothing,
tracks nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing. Nobody but you and Reddit sees what you do with it.

## What RESpawn keeps, and where

Everything stays in your own browser:

- **Your RESpawn settings:** switches, shortcuts and groups, user tags, hide lists. These are kept in
  the add-on's own browser storage, which Reddit's page cannot read.
- **Small records that make features work:** which posts you opened (for the "visited" filter), which
  subreddits you visit most (for "Most visited"), which comments you collapsed, when you last opened
  a post (for new-comment counts), your Reddit name and friends list (to color names), and a copy of
  RESpawn's on/off layout switches (so each page opens already arranged). These are kept
  in your browser's storage for reddit.com. Reddit's own page can read that storage, the same way it
  sees what you visit on its site. RESpawn never sends these records anywhere.
- **Light or dark:** the light/dark button and setting use Reddit's own Display Mode cookie, the same
  one Reddit's menu sets, so Reddit's pages open in the mode you picked.
- **The settings file:** only when you press Save under Settings > Backup, as a file on your computer.

Removing the add-on removes its own storage. The reddit.com records are cleared when you clear your
browser's site data for reddit.com.

## What RESpawn sends, and to whom

RESpawn only talks to Reddit, from your browser, signed in as you, and only when you use a feature:

- Votes, saves, hides, joining a subreddit and custom feeds (groups, FRIENDS, MOD) send the same
  requests Reddit's own buttons send.
- It reads from Reddit: your name, your friends list, the subreddits you moderate, your saved
  comments (for the Dashboard), and public profile and subreddit details when you point at a name.

When you open a video or picture from another website in place (YouTube, Vimeo, Streamable, TikTok,
Instagram, RedGifs, Imgur, Giphy), your browser loads it from that website, just as Reddit's own player
or a link would. Nothing loads from those sites until you open it.

RESpawn does not use analytics, crash reporting or any other outside service.

## Permissions

- **Access to www.reddit.com:** to add RESpawn's features to Reddit's pages.
- **Storage:** to keep your settings in your browser.
