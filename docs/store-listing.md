# Store listing (Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons)

Text and answers to paste into the two stores. Packages: `scripts/build.sh --store` makes
`dist/store/respawn-chrome-<version>.zip` and `dist/store/respawn-firefox-<version>.zip`.

## Name

RESpawn: RES features for New Reddit (both stores; Firefox allows 45 characters at most). The
description names Reddit Enhancement Suite in full, so searches for it still find RESpawn.

## Summary (132 characters at most)

Keeps the new Reddit look and brings back RES and old Reddit features: compact posts, filter bar, user tags, keyboard keys and more.

## Description

RESpawn keeps the new Reddit design and brings back the way old Reddit and Reddit Enhancement Suite (RES) worked.

Posts and feeds
- More posts on a screen, numbered, with votes, comments, share, save and hide on every row
- Sort tabs in the open, and RES's filter bar: nsfw, spoiler, visited, comments opened, expando, score
- Hide posts by words in the title, by subreddit or by website
- Pictures and videos open right in the feed, including Streamable, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Imgur and Giphy; a limit on videos playing at once
- Endless scrolling with page dividers and an optional pause every few pages
- Scores colored so the top posts on each page stand out; crossposts link to the original

Subreddits
- A shortcut bar across the top, with your own order, groups of subreddits and DASHBOARD, FRIENDS, MOD, MODQUEUE and SAVED links
- A switch for subreddit styles, on every subreddit or one at a time
- Hover cards for users and subreddits

Comments
- Remembered collapsed comments and new-comment counts since your last visit
- More replies load by themselves as you scroll; hide all replies with one button
- A navigator to jump between new comments, the poster's, moderators', friends', yours and people you tagged
- Colored names for the poster, moderators and friends

People
- Your own tags on anyone, in nine colors, and hiding a person's posts and comments

Also
- RES's keyboard shortcuts (press ? for the list)
- A dashboard with your saved comments, tags and hide lists
- Hide chat, ads, badges, "Recent posts" and suggestions; back-to-top button; hide your username on screen
- A one-click light and dark switch, or Auto to follow your computer; settings backup file

Not made by the RES team, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit. Free and open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/humangenome/RESpawn

## Store answers

- **Category:** Chrome: Social Networking. Firefox: Social & Communication.
- **Single purpose (Chrome):** Adds classic Reddit Enhancement Suite features to Reddit's new design on www.reddit.com.
- **Permission "storage":** Keeps the user's RESpawn settings (switches, shortcuts, tags, hide lists) in the browser.
- **Host access www.reddit.com:** RESpawn works only on Reddit's pages, where it adds its features to the page.
- **Remote code:** No. All code is in the package.
- **Data collected:** None. Nothing leaves the browser except the user's own actions on Reddit, sent to Reddit.
